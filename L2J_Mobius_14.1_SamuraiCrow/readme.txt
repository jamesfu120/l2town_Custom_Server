Lineage 2 - Samurai Crow
https://l2wiki.com/main/updates/3318.html

## New Samurai Class and Warg Class Updates
- A new class, Samurai, has been added, available only to male Kamael characters, with a unique appearance and combat style.
- Samurai uses a one-handed sword and has access to various skills, including Maneuver, Pursuit and Take Life, which deal damage and apply status effects to enemies.
- The Warg class has been updated with a speed increase effect to the Werewolf's Awakening skill, a new skill called Instinct Dash and enhancements to existing skills, such as Волчий Удар and Инстинктивный Рывок.
- The Warg class has new skills, including Wolf Sense and Warg's Tough Skin and modified effects for basic attacks and Enhanced Mark.

## Class Skill and Balance Adjustments
- The Tyrr Warrior class has increased max levels for some skills, new effects and modified enchant paths for attack skills.
- The Yul Archer class has removed regional changes, increased critical rate and modified skill cooldowns.
- The Wynn Summoner archetype has improved skills, including Ultimate Master Enhancement and Престиж Чернокнижника and modified servitor handling mastery skills.
- The Iss Enchanter archetype has a new skill, Accelerando and increased max levels for Blood Requiem and Deep Crazy Nocturne.
- The Feoh Wizard class has changed skill balance, added new skills and modified effects for Elemental Burst and Stance skills.

## Artifact and Talisman Enchantment Updates
- Artifact enchantment has been updated to allow enchanting up to +15, with various stat bonuses for each level.
- Two new talismans have been added: Talisman of Glory and Talisman of Radiance, each with unique effects and enchantment options.
- Orb of Fire and Orb of Air can now be enchanted up to +5 using Gran Kain's Scroll: Enchant Talisman Elysia, providing additional effects such as increased damage and resistance.

## Skill and Armor Enhancement Modifications
- The Wind Barrier skill has been updated with a new effect, recovering 20% of Max HP and creating a barrier that absorbs 150,000 damage.
- L-grade Armor can now be augmented using Chaos Augmenting Stone, with various possible effects such as increased P. Evasion, M. Evasion, P. Def and M. Def.
- The Radiant Knight's Circlet and Radiant Warrior's Circlet have been updated with new effects, including received P. Skill Critical Damage reduction and increased Basic Attack Power.

## Item Stat and Effect Adjustments
- The Silvery Brooch's M. Def. stat has been increased from 165 to 193.
- The Silvery Artifact Book can now be enchanted with Gran Kain's Scroll, adding 10 enchant levels with various stat bonuses.
- Dark Dragon's Accessories have been updated, including increased M. Def. values and a new "Perfection power +50" effect for +0 enchanted items.

## Kasha Orc Item Updates and Attack Power Changes
- Items related to Kasha Orcs have been renamed and updated, including changes to the Blood Rune Tainted by Chaos and its effects.
- Some items now increase all attack power, with updated effects listed for items such as Talismans and Masks.

## Sayha's Trust and Item Description Clarity
- Sayha's Trust now increases Basic Attack Power when equipped with Venir's Talisman or Soul Talisman of a certain level, with the increase varying by level.
- Item descriptions have been modified for clarity, including changes to "all attack power" and critical damage descriptions.

## New Hunting Zones and Collection Items
- New hunting zones, Snowy Volcano and Baluan Battleground, have been added with increased monster stats and unique attack strategies.
- Collection items can be obtained in the new hunting zones and Chaos Aether can be obtained with a certain chance by defeating monsters.

## Bug Fixes and New Instance Zones
- Various issues have been fixed, including problems with item visibility, cooldown animations and hunting zone difficulties.
- A new solo instance zone, Callax's Altar, has been added with varying difficulty levels and rewards, including Callax's Supply Pack containing items such as Talisman of Glory and Relic Tickets.

## Exalted Ability Path and Store Additions
- A new command channel instance zone, Baluan's Throne, has been added with a character level requirement of Lv. 115+ and a weekly entry limit.
- The Exalted ability path has been introduced, which can be unlocked using Exalted points obtained from completing specific quests.

## Quest Additions and UI Improvements
- Einhasad's Store now sells Angel's Accessories and party search notifications can be sent to chat.
- Various quests have been modified or added, including a new main quest in the Kasha Orc Fortress zone and side quests in the Snowy Volcano and Baluan Battleground hunting zones.
- Changes have been made to the UI, including improved auto-hunting and the display of effects on the XP/SP panel.

## Crafting, Exchange UI and Character Rankings
- Crafting for Talisman of Glory and Talisman of Radiance has been added with specific item chances and crafting fees.
- The exchange UI has been modified to display the level and class of characters, with friend, mentor, mentee, clan, or alliance members shown in green and others in red.
- Character rankings have been modified, with bonuses for high ranks in server and class-specific rankings, including stat increases and damage adjustments for various classes such as Knights, Warriors, Rogues and Mages.
