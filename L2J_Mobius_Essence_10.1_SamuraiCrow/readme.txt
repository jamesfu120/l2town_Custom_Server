Lineage 2 Essence - Samurai Crow
https://l2wiki.com/essence/updates/3326.html

## Introduction of the Samurai Class
- A new class, Samurai, has been introduced, which is a male Kamael character that deals physical melee damage and uses a Katana as their primary weapon.
- The Samurai class has various skills, including attack skills (e.g. Single Flash, Pursuit, Maneuver), buff skills (e.g. Wind, Forest, Fire) and passive skills (e.g. Katana Master, Samurai Armor Master).
- The Samurai class has unique features, such as special armor appearances, hairstyles and no shield usage.
- Samurai skills have been enhanced, including Intimidating Giant, Will to Victory, Heart of Sword and Fundamental Teaching.

## Titan Class Improvements
- Titan class has been improved with new skills, such as Hyper Rush and Titan Champion: Master and enhanced skills like Titanic Break and Titanic Sweep.
- Various Titan skills have been changed, including Destructive Shriek, Destroyer's Roar, Titans' Secrets and Titanic Weapon.
- The Guts skill has been updated with a maximum level of 10 and changed prices for learning.

## Skill Modifications and Updates
- The Frenzy skill has been modified, with changes to its MP consumption, P. Atk and other effects.
- The requirements for gaining Frenzy skill Lv. 8 have been changed, now requiring Magic Tablets (60 pcs.) instead of 300 million adena.
- The Cacophony of War and Cacophony of War: Master PvP skills have been updated, removing the Mark effect and adding HP/MP instant decrease and damage effects.
- The Ogre's Essence skill is now a buff, with removed penalty effects and can be dispelled using the Ultimate Dispel skill.
- The Blazing Strike skill has been updated, now hitting a single target, with expanded skill levels and a new effect for Lv. 9.
- The Life Force Harmony: Grand Khavatari skill effect has been changed to include a buff determined by Max HP, with increased effects based on current HP.

## Skill Enhancements for Multiple Classes
- The Burning Assault, Jump Attack and Double Hurricane Assault skills have been upgraded with increased Skill Power and expanded to Lv. 10.
- New skills have been added for the Duelist class, including Gleaming Blade and Crossed Blades, while some skills have been renamed or improved, such as Blade Strike and Amazing Blade Strike.
- The Challenger and Challenger: Master skills have been changed, with increased P. Atk., P. Skill Power and other effects.

## Buff and Debuff Adjustments
- The Doomcryer class has had buff skills combined and changed, with effects such as increased P. Atk., Received Critical Rate and Debuff/Anomaly Resistance.
- The Dominator class has also had buff skills combined and changed, with effects such as increased M. Skill Cooldown and M. Skill Critical Damage.
- The required level to learn Pa'agrio's Fist has been changed from 78 to 76.

## Skill Deletions and New Skill Additions
- Spirit Ore consumption for certain skills has been reduced, such as Pa'agrio's Power and Pa'agrio's Immunity.
- Certain skills have been deleted, including Pa'agrio's Glory, Pa'agrio's Vision, Pa'agrio's Haste and Hard Skin.
- New skills have been added, such as Song of Cosmos for Sword Muse and Dance of Cosmos for Spectral Dancer.

## Samurai Crow and Bard Class Updates
- The effects of certain skills have been changed, including Wild Beat, Crazy Waltz, Song of Silence and Dance of Medusa, with increased enchantment levels and Spirit Ore consumption.
- The Samurai Crow update enhances various skills for the Sword Muse and Spectral Dancer classes, including Amadeus, Flamenco, Cantabile and Vivace.
- New legendary skills, Cantabile and Vivace, have been added, which enhance Amadeus and Flamenco skills, respectively.
- Existing skills, such as Sword Symphony and Dance of Blood, have been changed and new effects have been added to Music Mastery and Dance Mastery skills.
- The Ultimate Defense: Bard skill effect has been changed and the Dark Elven Magic Affinity skill has been replaced by Dark Elves Revival.
- Various other changes and bug fixes have been made to skills related to the Bard class.

## Skill Mechanics and Effect Changes
- The 'Skill Critical Rate' effect is now part of the Unicorn's Friendship skill, not the Elemental Master skill.
- Bleeding Rose is now an active buff skill that increases the range of certain skills.
- New skills have been added, including Frosty Sting, Heart of Wind, Wind Mastery, Greater Wind Shot and Transcendent Greater Wind Shot.
- Existing skills have been modified, such as Dragon Strike, Time Burst (now Fire Explosion), Extra Hit, Elemental Change and Goring Charge.
- Some skills have been renamed, such as Elemental Wind (now Blessing of Winds) and Long Range Attack (now Wind Shooting).
- Elemental Mark has been deleted and Elemental Care has been changed to an AoE skill with increased HP restoration.

## Samurai Crow and Soul Hound Skill Enhancements
- Samurai Crow's abilities have been enhanced with increased attack power, PvE damage and maximum HP, as well as improved resistance to imprisonment and fear.
- Soul Hound's skills have been changed, with new passive skills added and some skills replaced or modified, such as Shining Transformation: Soul Hound and Shadow Transformation: Soul Hound.
- Phoenix Knight's skills have been updated, including increased skill range for Shield Strike and Knight's Assault and removal of fixed casting time for Shield Throwing.
- Changes have been made to various skills for the Samurai Crow class, including Shield Charge, Sanctuary and Chain Strike.
- New skills have been added, such as Phoenix Protection, Powerful Charge and Battle Will.

## Phoenix Knight and Hell Knight Class Updates
- The Phoenix Shield skill effect has been changed and the Divine Templar and Hell Knight classes have also received updates to their skills, including Noblesse Circlet and Knight's Power.
- New skills have been added, including Hell Smashing, Powerful Charge, Battle Will, Dark Might and Dark Knight's Break, with various effects such as increased damage, stun rates and debuffs.
- The amount of Spirit Ore consumed by skills depends on the probability and enchant value, with different classes having unique consumption rates.

## Rogue Archetype and Dragon Weapon Introductions
- Improvements have been made to Rogue archetype skills, including Lethal Blow and Dark Blow and the Backstab skill now has a higher chance of hitting and a 100% chance of hitting a vulnerable spot when attacking from behind.
- A new Dragon Weapon, Staff, has been added with unique effects and stats that increase depending on the enchant value and trigger rates for skills have been listed for each enchant value.
- Dragon Weapons can be created using a special craft with various materials and adena costs.
- The appearance of Dragon Weapons can be changed using a special craft, with the inheritance of augmenting/enchantment level depending on the material used.
- The Immortal Ring can be enchanted using the Scroll: Enchant Rare Accessories.

## Spirit Rulers' Agathions and Spellbook Changes
- The Immortal Ring has 5 enchantment levels, with effects including increased P./ M. Atk., P./ M. Def. and All Critical Rate.
- The ring cannot be enchanted with Improved and Stable Scrolls: Enchant Rare Accessories and equipping two identical rings only applies the effect of one ring.
- Spirit Rulers' Agathions, including Fire Spirit King Ignis, Water Spirit Queen Nebula, Wind Spirit King Procella and Earth Spirit King Petram, have been added with various effects and can be created by compounding attribute agathions.
- Brooches, Talisman Bracelets and Agathion Summon Bracelets have had their stats changed, with all registered items being removed from the World Trade and returned to the private warehouse.
- New spellbooks have been added, including Spellbook: Hyper Rush, Spellbook: Titan Champion: Master, Spellbook: Crossed Blades and others, which can be dropped by various bosses.
- Some spellbooks have been replaced or changed, such as Spellbook: Lunatic Crusher being replaced by Spellbook: Dark Knight's Break.

## Item and Equipment Enhancements
- Aden's Soul Crystal effects have been improved, with new effects added, including P./ M. Def., P./ M. Atk. and acquired XP/ SP stats.
- Common, enhanced, superior and rare spellbooks have been merged into one book of each type, allowing skills corresponding to different ranks to be acquired with the same book.
- The Heavenly Cloak enchant's Property 3 now includes an acquired XP/SP effect.
- The enchant success rate of Agathion Valakas and Gran Kain's Pendant has been increased.
- A new Guardian's Locket item has been added to the guardians' equipment, with effects varying by enchant value.

## Pet and Guardian System Updates
- The Pet's Protection levels have been extended to Lv. 3, with changed effects for each level.
- The drop rate of certain items, including Talisman and Circlet packs, has been decreased.
- Guardians' equipment and skills have been improved, including increased P. Atk. and M. Atk. and the addition of transcendent skills.

## New Events and Hunting Zones
- The Antharas Raid now has a Party Search feature on Sundays at 22:00, with a maximum of 50 characters recruited within 5 minutes.
- Completing the Antharas raid rewards players with Red Lanterns, Dragon Rune, Lucky Pack, Antharas' Treasure Chest and Antharas' Glittering Treasure Chest, depending on the boss' HP percentage and participation.
- New hunting zones have been added, including Horong's Bamboo Forest and Fire Dragon's Swamp, where players can acquire Augmenting Stones, Blessed Augmenting Stones and other items.
- A new instance zone, Contest Forest, has been added, where up to 8 players can compete in 1v1 rounds to earn points by killing special monsters and receive rewards, including Duel Treasure Chests.
- Final participants in a match can receive rewards, including Duel Treasure Chests, based on the competition results.

## Quest and Mission Additions
- A new Daily Mission, "Battle in the Contest Forest" and "Battle at Camille's Gate", have been added with rewards such as Red Lantern, Duel Treasure Chest and Camille's Treasure Chest.
- A new instance zone, Camille's Gate, has been added with a matching system, where up to 5 participants can enter and battle against Camille for rewards.
- Changes have been made to Special Zones, including the addition of Mafia's Secret Hideout, changes to the Celestial Tower and removal of certain hunting zones like Fafurion's Nest and Cyrax's Temple.
- The player who deals the final blow to Mafia Leader Luciano is rewarded with Mafia Leader Luciano's Mysterious Box, which contains various items.
- Mysterious Golden Coins can be obtained by exchanging Bloodstained Coins and can be used to purchase items such as Package: Doll Summon Coupon - Mafia Leader Luciano and Mafia's Secret Hideout's Treasure Chest.
- New special hunting zones have been added, including Mother Tree's Orphanage, with entry level requirements and usage times specified.
- New quests have been added, including the "1st Transcendent Challenge" to the "10th Transcendent Challenge", where players can defeat Transcendent Specimens to earn rewards in XP and SP.

## Server and Store Updates
- New missions have been added, such as "Hellish Training in a Transcendent Instance Zone I", "II" and "III", which require players to defeat specific Transcendent Specimens to earn rewards.
- A new secret dungeon called the Wild Beast Reserve has been added, where players can kill Animal Guardians to obtain Guardian's Essence and other rewards.
- The Monster Invasion event has been updated, with new hunting zones and rewards for defeating Monster Lords and Giant Orc Amadu.
- New events have been added, including the "Wolfmen Checkpoint" and "Dwelling of Spirits", which offer unique rewards and challenges for players.
- Defeating bosses in the Dwelling of Spirits yields rewards, including rings, earrings, spellbooks and evolution stones, which can be distributed using the Raid Auction System.
- Changes have been made to hunting zones, including increased monster numbers, changed rewards and added buffs for melee classes in certain zones.
- A new server, Samurai, is opening with unique features, such as separate 3V3 Olympiad fights, limited PvP hours and exclusive buffs during active PvP hours.
- The L-Coin store has been reorganized, with new products added, including coupons for +5 boss weapons and special armor, as well as packages for accessories and guardians.
- New items have been added to the L-Coin Store and Clan Shop, including Doll Summon Coupons and scrolls to boost attack and defense.

## Doll Collections and Game Mechanics
- The number of monsters required to complete certain quests has been decreased, including "Mutated Giants' Research" and "Protect Hot Springs Nepenthes" quests.
- New quests for Samurais have been added, with rewards including XP, Soulshots and equipment such as the Earring of Strength and Ring of Wisdom.
- The document 'Samurai Crow — Knowledge base Lineage II Essence' provides information on various doll collections, including Reptiles, Mere Lizards, Dragon Swordsman, Knife Masters, Good Intentions, Aqua Friends, Mafia's Secret Hideout and Dice Master, each with unique effects.
- Dolls have different sorts, such as Common, Enhanced, Superior, Rare, Heroic and Legendary, with varying combat power, stats and acquired XP/SP bonuses.
- Changes have been made to mail services, including a fee for sending adena by paid mail, a limit of 3 letters per day and changes to common craft items and slots.

## Mail Services and Crafting Adjustments
- The common craft items have been updated with new items and changes to existing ones, including Adena packs, Soulshot Tickets and Enchant Scrolls.
- The prices for common craft have changed, with the crafting fee now 3,000,000 Adena on regular servers and 1,000,000 Adena on Essence servers.
- Updates have been made to Dyes, including changes to stats and compounding fees, with decreased fees for Dyes Lv. 1-30 and reduced crafting prices for Dye enhancement items.
- Enchantment Scroll targets have been added for certain items, including Circlet of Hero, Phantom Mask and Talisman of Baium.

## Class History and UI Changes
- Next Target settings have been changed to select all hostile targets, including Chaotic characters.
- Class change history now tracks the acquisition of Heroic/Legendary skills and the cost for extracting these skills has been changed to Aden's Blessing [Sealed] 1 pc.
- Game Assistant has been reorganized and its icon has been removed from the right side UI panel.

## Clan and Teleportation Adjustments
- Clan changes include a new default Crest, removal of clan level requirements to join an Alliance and a reduction in the number of Clans in an Alliance from 3 to 2.
- Teleportation cost is temporarily set to 0 adena on the Samurai server.
- A bug causing enemy marks to display on clan members has been fixed.

## Bug Fixes and Final Updates
- A cooldown has been added to Elixir.
- Characters now receive 1 additional stats point at Lv. 60 and Lv. 70.
